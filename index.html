Longshot helps you assess web applications you own or are authorized to test. Connect a signed-in browser tab, discover inputs, run bounded checks, inspect findings and export your review.

Your browser session, your tools

The optional native HTTP proxy lets separately installed tools send their own requests with configurable browser cookie/header overrides. Browser Bridge requires a local companion and explicit pairing.

Clear coverage and limits

Longshot has its own scanner. External ZAP remains a separate engine. Not every OWASP Top 10 flaw can be detected automatically; authorization, business logic and sensitive-data/payment applications need professional assessment.
